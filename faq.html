Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about our restaurant and self-ordering system.

Feel free to browse through the questions below and find the answers you need.

Q: How do I place an order using the self-ordering system?

A: To place an order using our self-ordering system, simply navigate to the order page and select your desired items from the menu. You can customize your order and submit it directly from your table.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?

A: We accept various payment methods, including credit/debit cards, mobile payments, and cash. You can choose your preferred payment option during the checkout process.

Q: How long does it take to receive my order?

A: Orders are typically ready within 10-15 minutes after placement. If you experience any delays, please notify our staff immediately.

Q: Can I modify my order after submitting it?

A: Yes, you can modify your order before it is confirmed. Please notify our staff immediately if you need to make any changes.
